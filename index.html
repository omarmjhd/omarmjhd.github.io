About Me

I'm a Product Manager focused on building user-facing products that move metrics users actually feel.

These days I'm at Indeed in Austin, Texas, leading Matching, the systems that connect job seekers with the right opportunities at scale. Before Matching, I led product strategy for Recommendations, the team building Indeed's job recommendation systems for hundreds of millions of job seekers. I got my start in product as a Research PM in SEO and Growth, after a few years as a Research Engineer on the same team. It's a transition I'd recommend to any engineer curious about product.

Before Indeed, I was a Software Engineer I and II at Bazaarvoice, building APIs that helped retailers understand their customers. I started there straight out of school after interning at Harris Corporation, Boeing, JPMorgan Chase, and a small Atlanta startup called VeriSolutions.

I graduated from the Georgia Institute of Technology in December 2016 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Economics.

AI tooling has become a real part of how I work. I use Claude Code, agentic workflows, and prompt engineering to prototype faster, accelerate analysis work, and ship things I'd otherwise wait on. It's a productivity multiplier I won't give up.

I've been in Austin nearly a decade now, by way of Atlanta. When I'm not at work or on side projects, I'm at concerts, on a golf course, or both.
